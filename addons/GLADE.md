# GLADE.md

## Overview
A low-poly rhythm action game where players destroy an incoming barrage of explosive notes by slicing them in real time before they collide with a spinning energy core. The closer the notes get, the faster they move—demanding precise timing and smooth dragging inputs.

## Genre & Core Mechanic
**Genre:** Rhythm Video Game (Action/Timing).  
**Core Mechanic:** Explosive notes travel toward a central, spinning energy core. Notes deal damage on impact; if enough damage accumulates, the core is destroyed. Players **click-and-drag to slice notes** away. Notes **accelerate** as they approach the core, increasing difficulty late in each phrase.
- **Slice Notes:** Single pass required—slice once with a clean drag track to remove them.
- **Long Slice Notes:** Extended targets that require **repeated slicing** along their length (e.g., multiple drag segments) to fully clear them.
Notes should be visually readable at a glance and synchronized to the music beat so players can “aim with the rhythm.”

## Art Style & Tone
**Art Style:** Low Poly, stylized geometry, bright materials on the energy core, and crisp silhouettes for incoming notes.  
**Tone:** Intense and arcade-fast—impact VFX, readable telegraphs, and a high-energy visual rhythm consistent with acceleration toward the core.

## Key Design Pillars
- **Beat-synced readability:** Clear timing windows and strong audio-visual synchronization.
- **Progressive pressure:** Increasing note speed and density as notes approach the core.
- **Input satisfaction:** Smooth, responsive slice dragging with satisfying hit/clear feedback.
- **Clear note taxonomy:** Distinct visuals and interaction rules for slice vs long slice notes.

## Player Experience Goals
- Enable players to consistently slice to reduce core damage while learning patterns.
- Deliver a “flow” rhythm feel: drag timing aligns with beats, not guesswork.
- Provide escalating challenge through speed and long-note handling without unfair deaths.
